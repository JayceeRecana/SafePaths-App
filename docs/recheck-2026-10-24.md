## Finding fixed

**Device registration could be missed after email verification.** Notification setup watched sign-in/sign-out events, while token storage deliberately skips unverified accounts. Verifying the same signed-in account does not require another sign-in, so a stable device token could remain unregistered until restart or a later sign-in. This could prevent targeted report-reply notifications from reaching that device.

- Notification registration now watches verified, non-anonymous `userChanges()` events, including account refreshes.
- The online account gate retries token registration after the verified server profile is ready, covering profile creation/recovery. This happens without delaying entry into the app.
- The gate discards a late profile result if its screen has closed or the current account has changed before caching the role or opening the home screen.
- Two regression checks cover verification of the same signed-in account and retry after a temporary registration failure. Signed-out, anonymous and unverified states do not trigger registration through this listener.

This fixes registration timing; notification permission, server acceptance and successful device delivery remain separate conditions.
